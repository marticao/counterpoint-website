# Counterpoint: Reading the Understudied

Website for **Counterpoint: Reading the Understudied**, an interdisciplinary reading group at The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK).

Counterpoint explores overlooked histories, languages, environments, technologies, communities, and forms of knowledge, with a particular interest in Global South perspectives. The group provides a space for participants from different disciplines and backgrounds to place different perspectives into conversation through readings, research discussions, and other exchanges.

## Website

**https://counterpointcuhk.org**

The website contains information about:

- the Counterpoint reading group
- the 2026–27 programme
- current and selected readings
- past sessions and related reflections
- resources
- upcoming sessions and registration

Session reflections and updates are also shared through **Re:Code** on Substack and Instagram.
