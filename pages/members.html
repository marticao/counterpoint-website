Members

Different disciplines, backgrounds, and perspectives.

Counterpoint is shaped by members from different fields and cultural backgrounds, each bringing their own questions, experiences, and ways of approaching the understudied.

01

Mar Lorence Ticao

History

Coordinator

Mar is a historian and architect who spends a great deal of time reading old newspapers and maps. Her work explores Manila through stories of fires, floods, inequality, everyday life, and attempts to remake the city. She is fascinated by how the past keeps showing up in the present. She also experiments with digital tools to find new ways of exploring, connecting, and telling these stories.

Urban History · Colonial Manila · Environmental History · Digital Humanities · Public History

02

Misa Cheung

Anthropology

Misa is trained in anthropology and is interested in the phenomenon of baan gung (扮工, “pretending to work”) in Hong Kong offices. She hopes to explore how the ethics of work can become a game of imitation rather than producing tangible outcomes, and what this might reveal about the capitalist economic system.

Office Culture · Work Ethics · Performative Act · BL · Multicultural Education · AI Education

03

Cory HUANG Xiadong

History

Cory is a PhD candidate in the Department of History at CUHK. He is currently working on a dissertation, Creolizing the World in an Island: Changing Cosmopolitanism in Nineteenth-Century Mauritius. His research interests focus on the history of South China, Southeast Asia, and the Indian Ocean.

Indian Ocean · Tropical Islands · South China · Intercultural Studies · Decoloniality

04

Carleon Mendoza

Linguistics

Carleon is a PhD candidate in Linguistics at CUHK. Born and raised in Hong Kong, his research bridges theoretical linguistics and real-world application, specializing in heritage languages, multilingual acquisition, and morphosyntax. Drawing on his experience as an educator, he advocates for linguistic diversity and cultural identity, engaging students, parents, local Chinese children, ethnic minorities, and the broader community.

Heritage Languages · Multilingual Acquisition · Morphosyntax

06

Carlos Pardo Pérez

History

Carlos is from Spain and is currently an MPhil student at CUHK. His research focuses on Philippine history from the early modern period to the late nineteenth century, including legal history and relations between Japan, Spain, and the Philippines. He holds a bachelor's degree in History and a master's degree in Education. He is particularly interested in intercultural and transnational encounters, translation, and communication through time.

Philippine History · Transnational History · Literature · Languages · Psychology

07

Yanyu Sun

Architectural History

Yanyu is a writer and researcher based between Hong Kong and Shenzhen. With a background in architectural history and design, her work explores cities through their everyday spaces, objects, infrastructure, and cultures.

Architectural History · Material culture · Urban culture · Urban Infrastructure

Michael Lane Tessmer

Higher Education

Michael is an assistant lecturer at CUHK and a PhD candidate in the Faculty of Education, University of Hong Kong. His research interests include identity, postgraduate education, and comparative politics and higher education policy.

Higher Education Policy & Practice · Comparative Politics · Graduate Education · Identity

Dannie LEI Danyang

Architecture & Urban Studies

Dannie holds a PhD in Architecture from CUHK, as well as a bachelor's degree in Landscape Architecture and a master's degree in Urban Design. Her research examines age-friendly neighbourhoods and older migrants' social integration, with a focus on how everyday urbanism shapes ageing and mobility experiences in high-rise, high-density urban settings.

Ageing · Neighbourhoods · Social Inclusion · High-density Cities
