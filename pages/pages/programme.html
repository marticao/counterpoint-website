Programme

Reading, research, and conversation throughout 2026–27.

Counterpoint meets approximately once a month during the academic year for participant-led book discussions, research exchanges, informal gatherings, and a guest conversation.

2026

01

Planning & Introductory Meeting

Starting the Conversation

Start-of-term social gathering introducing the group and its aims, discussing the idea of “reading the understudied,” sharing members’ research interests, and finalizing the reading list and schedule.

02

Book Discussion

Language Diversity, Cultural Memory & Endangered Languages

A reading conversation focused on language diversity, cultural memory, and endangered languages.

Register for this session →
03

Book Discussion

Historical Narratives, Memory & Knowledge Production

A discussion focused on historical narratives, memory, and the production of knowledge.

04

Research Roundtable & Term Reflection

Term 1 Reflection

Members share work-in-progress, research ideas, or ongoing projects, followed by discussion and feedback, alongside an end-of-term gathering and reflection on the first term’s conversations.

2027

05

Research Roundtable & Planning Meeting

Research in Conversation

The group reconvenes to exchange ideas on current research, methodological questions, and emerging projects, while identifying shared interests and directions for the term ahead.

06

Book Discussion

Environmental Thought, Colonialism & Ecological Justice

A discussion focused on environmental thought, colonialism, and ecological justice.

07

Book Discussion

Technology, Material Culture, Recycling & Everyday Histories

A discussion on the intersection of technology, material culture, recycling, and everyday histories.

08

Guest Lecture & Discussion

Guest Conversation

A guest lecture and discussion with a visiting scholar, followed by a Q&A session.

09

Fiction Reading Discussion & Research Roundtable

Identity, Migration, Memory & Lived Experience

A fiction discussion alongside an exchange on members’ work-in-progress, research ideas, and methods, followed by group feedback.

10

Closing Meeting

Looking Back, Looking Forward

Reflection on the year’s readings and activities, followed by a conversation about possible future directions.
